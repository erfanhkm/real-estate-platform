Richmond Neighbourhood Guide

A flat, riverside city south of Vancouver on the Canada Line — widely known for its restaurants and food scene.

Character

Richmond occupies islands at the mouth of the Fraser River: flat, largely suburban, and famous across the region for its Asian restaurants, food courts and shopping. The city centre along No. 3 Road is the urban spine, while Steveston, in the southwest, keeps a fishing-village character with its docks and heritage buildings. Vancouver International Airport (YVR) sits on Sea Island, within Richmond.

Getting around

The Canada Line runs from Richmond–Brighouse and Aberdeen stations to downtown Vancouver and the airport. Buses feed the stations from across the city, and the flat terrain makes cycling practical for local trips.

Homes

Mainly single-family houses and townhouses, plus low-rise condos — with taller condo towers concentrated near the Canada Line stations in the city centre.

Who it suits

Families who value flat, bikeable streets and local schools; frequent air travellers; and buyers who want suburban space with a direct SkyTrain link downtown.
